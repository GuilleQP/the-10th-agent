Spiral-of-Silence Benchmark

Each experiment pits 9 agents sharing a wrong belief against 1 locked dissenter holding ground truth. The headline metric is the truth-conversion rate: the fraction of the majority that adopted the dissenter's correct position by the end — green means the majority moved toward truth, red means the dissenter was silenced.

TL;DR — Nine AI agents get a false belief; one locked “dissenter” gets the truth and never backs down. They debate, and we measure whether the majority is converted to the truth or the lone correct voice gets drowned out (the spiral of silence). Score = truth-conversion rate: the share of the majority that comes around.
